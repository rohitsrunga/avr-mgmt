Employee Handbook

 

1.0 Welcome

Welcome to . We're excited to have you on board. Our team plays an essential role in creating a comfortable, clean, and welcoming environment for every guest we serve. This handbook will guide you through the basic policies, expectations, and benefits of your employment. We look forward to building something great together.

This handbook is a general guide and not a contract. LLC may change, update, or remove any part of this handbook at any time. We will make an effort to communicate important changes when they occur.

1.2 At-Will Employment

Employment with LLC is "at-will." This means either you or the company may end the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause or notice. Only a written agreement signed by the owner can change this at-will relationship.

1.3 Equal Employment Opportunity

LLC is an equal opportunity employer. We provide equal employment opportunities to all employees and applicants without regard to race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or genetics. This policy applies to all terms and conditions of employment, including recruiting, hiring, placement, promotion, termination, layoff, recall, transfer, leaves of absence, compensation, and training.

2.0 General Policies

2.1 Ethics Code

We expect all employees to act with honesty, integrity, and professionalism at all times. You represent our company to guests, coworkers, and the community — and we want that representation to reflect trust, fairness, and respect. You are expected to:

  • Follow all laws and company policies
  • Protect confidential company and guest information
  • Avoid conflicts of interest
  • Communicate honestly and respectfully

Any violation of our ethics code may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

3.0 Hiring and Orientation

3.1 Introductory Period

Your first 90 days of employment are considered an introductory period. This time helps both you and the company evaluate your fit for the role. During this period, we will assess your performance, attendance, professionalism, and overall contribution to the team. Completion of the introductory period does not guarantee continued employment. Your at-will status remains in effect throughout and after this period.

4.0 Wage and Hour Policies

4.1 Attendance

Being on time and present for your scheduled shifts is essential to the smooth operation of our hotel. If you're going to be late or absent, please notify your supervisor as soon as possible — preferably before your scheduled start time. Frequent or unexcused absences may lead to disciplinary action. Approved vacation, sick leave, and legal leave are not considered violations of this policy.

4.2 Business Expenses

If you incur business-related expenses (such as purchasing supplies or running work-related errands), please get pre-approval from your supervisor. Submit a completed expense report with original receipts for reimbursement. Include the date, amount, and business purpose of each expense.

4.3 Direct Deposit

We strongly encourage direct deposit. You can elect or change direct deposit using the form included in your onboarding packet.

4.6 Paycheck Deductions

We are required by law to deduct federal and state taxes, Social Security, and other legally mandated items from your paycheck. You may also authorize voluntary deductions, such as for benefits or uniforms. If you believe there is an error or unauthorized deduction, report it to your supervisor immediately. Any verified mistake will be corrected by your next scheduled payday. You will never be penalized for reporting a payroll issue.

4.7 Recording Time

All non-exempt (hourly) employees must accurately record the time they begin and end work, including meal breaks. Use the hotel's time clock or designated timekeeping system.

  • Clock in just before your shift starts and clock out immediately after it ends.
  • Do not work off the clock or falsify time records.
  • Report any missed punches or timekeeping issues to your supervisor immediately.

Falsifying time records or working unauthorized hours may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

4.8 Meal and Rest Breaks

Employees working 6 or more consecutive hours are entitled to a 30-minute unpaid break. Let your supervisor know if you are unable to take your break.

5.0 Performance, Discipline & Termination

5.2 Open Door

We want to maintain a positive and respectful workplace. If you ever experience a problem or concern, we encourage you to speak directly with your supervisor. If the issue isn't resolved or involves your supervisor, you may bring the concern to the General Manager. We are committed to addressing concerns fairly, privately, and without retaliation.

5.3 Resignation

If you decide to leave your position, we ask that you notify your supervisor in writing as soon as possible. This helps ensure a smooth transition for your team. You must return all company property (uniforms, keys, etc.) on or before your last day. Failure to return company property may result in deductions from your final paycheck, with the employee's written authorization and as permitted by Maine law. Please keep your contact information updated so we can mail your final pay and tax forms if needed.

5.4 Standards of Conduct

We are committed to providing a respectful, safe, and professional work environment for all employees, guests, and partners. By working at LLC, you agree to follow basic workplace standards. Violations of conduct may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

6.0 General Policies & Code of Ethics

6.2 Personal Appearance

Your appearance represents the company. All employees are expected to report to work neat, clean, and dressed appropriately for their role. Uniforms must be worn if provided, and personal grooming should be maintained daily. Avoid excessive use of perfume or cologne. If your appearance is inappropriate for work, your supervisor may ask you to go home and return properly dressed.

6.2.1 Visitors in the Workplace

To maintain a safe and professional environment, employees may not bring children, family members, or personal visitors to the workplace during work hours. No one other than authorized hotel staff should be in the back office, laundry, kitchen, or guest areas. Exceptions require prior approval from the General Manager.

6.3 Personal Cell Phone / Mobile Device Use

You may bring your personal phone or device to work, but it should not interfere with your duties.

  • Use phones only during breaks or mealtimes unless it's an emergency.
  • Keep phones on silent or vibrate while on shift.
  • Do not take calls, record, or take photos in guest areas or during work tasks.
  • The use of earphones, earbuds, or wireless pods is strictly prohibited while on duty.

6.6 Third-Party Disclosures

If someone from the media, legal system, or other outside party contacts you about the company, do not speak on the company's behalf. Refer all such inquiries to the General Manager or the owner. This ensures accurate and consistent communication.

6.7 Workplace Privacy and Right to Inspect

All company property — including lockers, computers, phones, workstations, and vehicles — belongs to the company and may be inspected at any time without notice. Do not expect personal privacy when using or storing items in company property. Personal belongings brought to work are your responsibility.

7.0 Employee Benefits and Protections

7.1 Military Leave (USERRA)

We comply with all state and federal laws regarding military service. If you are called to active duty or military training, notify your supervisor as soon as possible and provide supporting documents. You will be reinstated to your position (or a similar one) upon return, in accordance with the law.

7.2 Paid Time Off (PTO)

Employees earn 1 hour of paid leave for every 40 hours worked, up to 40 hours per year, as required by Maine law. You can begin using PTO after 120 days of employment.

7.6 Jury Duty Leave

You may take time off for jury duty. Notify your supervisor as soon as you receive a summons. Exempt (salaried) employees will not have pay reduced for partial-week absences.

7.7 Voting Leave

If your schedule does not allow time to vote, you may request time off to do so. Requests should be made in advance and coordinated with your supervisor.

7.8 Equal Employment & Non-Harassment Policy

We are an equal opportunity employer. We do not tolerate discrimination or harassment based on race, color, religion, national origin, sex, gender identity, age, disability, pregnancy, marital status, veteran status, or any other protected category. Harassment of any kind — verbal, physical, or visual — is strictly prohibited. If you feel harassed or witness inappropriate behavior, report it to your supervisor, the General Manager, HR, or the owner immediately. Your report will be taken seriously, and retaliation is not allowed.

7.9 Religious Accommodation

We respect employees' religious beliefs and practices. If you need time off or a change in schedule due to religious observance, please contact your supervisor and we will work with you to find a reasonable solution, unless it causes undue hardship.

8.0 Safety and Loss Prevention

8.2 General Safety

Safety is everyone's responsibility. Always follow posted safety procedures and report any unsafe conditions, hazards, or injuries to your supervisor right away. If you are injured at work, no matter how small, report it immediately so we can respond and document the incident properly. Failure to follow safety rules may result in disciplinary action.

8.3 Workplace Violence

We have zero tolerance for violence, threats, or intimidation in the workplace. Prohibited conduct includes:

  • Physical aggression or fighting
  • Verbal threats or abusive language
  • Intimidating behavior or gestures
  • Possession of weapons (unless legally authorized and approved)

If you witness or experience any violent behavior, report it immediately to your supervisor or the General Manager. We will investigate all reports and take appropriate action. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is strictly prohibited.

9.0 Trade Secrets and Confidentiality

As part of your job, you may have access to confidential information, including guest records, business strategies, pricing, and operating practices. You agree to keep this information confidential during and after your employment. Unauthorized disclosure may result in disciplinary action and legal consequences.

10.0 Customer Relations

All employees should have a basic understanding of the hotel's services, amenities, and layout. Knowing how to answer common questions like check-in time, breakfast hours, Wi-Fi access, or directions helps create a better guest experience and reflects positively on our team. You may refer to our website or ask your supervisor if you ever have any questions regarding our services.

  • The company may revise this handbook at any time.
  • I should ask my supervisor or the General Manager if I have any questions.
